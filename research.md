## Relevant Industry Standards or Protocols

- **EPA FIFRA (Federal Insecticide, Fungicide, and Rodenticide Act)** — Governs pesticide registration, labelling, and use; software must support label-compliance and product-application recording
- **EPA Worker Protection Standard (WPS), 40 CFR Part 170** — Requires training records, re-entry interval tracking, and chemical-application documentation for agricultural and structural pest control
- **State Pesticide Applicator Licensing** — All US states require licensed applicator credentials; software must track technician certifications and licence expiry
- **NPMA (National Pest Management Association) Standards** — Industry best-practice guidelines for service documentation, integrated pest management (IPM), and quality assurance
- **GreenPro / QualityPro Certification** — Industry certification programmes requiring documented IPM practices and environmental stewardship
- **DOT Hazmat Rules (49 CFR)** — Transportation regulations for restricted-use pesticides carried in service vehicles

## Market Research

**Market Size:** The US pest control industry generates over $20 billion in annual revenue; software penetration is high — over 65% of pest control companies are expected to use specialised management software by 2026. The global pest control software market is growing in line with the broader field-service-management market.

**Funding:** WorkWave (PestPac parent) is PE-backed (PSG Equity); FieldRoutes was acquired by ServiceTitan; GorillaDesk is privately held; Pocomos is bootstrapped. The market is consolidating around FSM platform acquirers.

**Pricing Landscape:** SMB tools (GorillaDesk, Jobber) start at $49–$150/month; mid-market platforms run $200–$800/month for a small fleet; enterprise solutions (ServiceTitan, PestPac for large operators) involve custom contracts.

**Key Buyer Personas:** Pest control business owners, operations managers, service technicians, compliance officers in larger companies, and franchise operators managing multi-location fleets.

**Notable Trends:** Sustainability and IPM (integrated pest management) mandates driving demand for chemical-usage minimisation tracking; increasing regulatory scrutiny of pesticide application records; customer demand for real-time technician tracking and digital service reports; route density optimisation reducing fuel and technician costs; growing importance of customer-review management for local SEO.

## AI-Native Opportunity

- AI-driven route optimisation clustering appointments by geography and service type to maximise stops per day while minimising drive time
- Predictive re-treatment scheduling using property history, weather data, and infestation patterns to recommend optimal return intervals per account
- Chemical-usage intelligence flagging over-application, suggesting lower-risk alternatives, and generating EPA-compliant application records automatically
- Computer-vision identification of pest species from technician-uploaded photos to recommend treatment protocols and flag reportable infestations
- Customer churn prediction identifying accounts at risk of cancellation based on service-call history, complaint patterns, and engagement metrics
